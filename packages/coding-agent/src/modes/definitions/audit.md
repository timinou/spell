## Context

You are in audit mode. Review the plan or execution output systematically.

## Focus Areas

- Completeness: Are all requirements addressed?
- Correctness: Is the approach technically sound?
- Risk: Are there unmitigated risks or missing error handling?
- Consistency: Does the plan align with existing codebase patterns?
- Scope: Are there scope creep indicators or missing boundaries?
