** ITEM BUG-414 fixed: code_graph_cache was never invalidated on filesystem changes — edge queries returned stale results from the first-build graph. Root cause: FileWatcher had no subscriber API; invalidate_for_file existed but nobody called it.

Fix (3 files, commit 11e3a7bb3):
1. watcher.rs: +subscribers field + on_change() + dispatch in handle_event (catch_unwind guarded)
2. lib.rs: watcher.on_change(Box::new(|p| code_graph_cache::invalidate_for_file(p))) on buffer_registry init
3. code_graph_cache_e2e.rs: 4 tests (write→invalidate→rebuild, multi-cycle, unrelated path)

Design decision: callback over bounded channel. invalidate_for_file is O(1) DashMap retain, 1 subscriber, no overflow risk — simpler than a crossbeam channel + background thread.
