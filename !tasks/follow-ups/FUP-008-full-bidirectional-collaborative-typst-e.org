* Scope
- Agent and human edit the same Typst document simultaneously with conflict resolution
- Operational transform or CRDT for concurrent edits
- Cursor presence indicators
- Section locking to prevent edit collisions
- Real-time diff visualization

* Rationale for Deferral
MVP agent-first + human-tweak workflow (agent drafts, human adjusts) is sufficient for V1. Full collaborative editing requires OT or CRDT infrastructure that does not exist in the current stack. This is a significant engineering investment justified only after V1 validates the workflow pattern.
