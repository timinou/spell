* Context (D3 W3 reviewer swarm, P3)

`TransactionJournal` records `startedAt` (ms) in every JournalRecord but `sweep()`
never reads it. After the P1/P2 fixes (liveness probe + per-launch nonce), recovery is
gated correctly by pid-liveness \u2014 a DEAD pid's journal is recovered, a LIVE one
skipped. `startedAt` is currently dead weight.

Two coherent options:
A. DROP startedAt \u2014 the liveness probe fully decides recovery; the field reads as an
   unfinished staleness intent.
B. USE startedAt as a secondary staleness gate \u2014 e.g. only auto-recover a dead-pid
   journal older than a small grace window, and surface very-recent ones for manual
   inspection (defends against a race where a process died microseconds ago and a fast
   restart sweeps a journal whose files might still be settling). Low marginal value
   given the pid-liveness probe already handles the live case.

Recommend A (drop it) unless a concrete staleness need appears \u2014 the liveness probe is
the correct primary gate and startedAt adds nothing today.
