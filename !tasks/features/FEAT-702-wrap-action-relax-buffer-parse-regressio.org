* Intention & Purpose
Make `wrap` actually work. Currently every wrap (even `["if(true){", "$BODY", "}"]`) fails with "structurally invalid" because the post-wrap full-buffer reparse trips on edge cases. Replace the full-file reparse with a localized parse of just the wrapped subtree.

* Context & Narrative
`crates/pi-code-engine/src/buffer.rs:868-875` re-parses the entire post-wrap buffer with tree-sitter and rejects when `tree.root_node().has_error()`. Wrap templates that produce locally-valid but globally-suspect ASTs trip the check. The intended semantic: "the edit must not introduce a parse error at or inside the wrap site". Outside the wrap site, errors pre-existed and aren't our concern.

- Wrap region spans multiple top-level nodes: use lowest common ancestor
- Wrap region is entire file: subtree check = full check
