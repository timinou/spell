* Design Decisions

1. **plan-mode-active.md** — This is the fattest overlay at 2,504 words (387 lines). Key compression targets:
   - The "design decision change" =<critical>= block appears identically in 3 conditional branches (default workflow, iterative, ultraplan). Deduplicate: emit it once after all branches close.
   - Org Item Body Standard (ultraplan mode): the sub-outline example is verbose — compress to pattern + 1 example.
   - Phase descriptions: each phase is 2-4 lines of prose. Compress to 1 line notation per phase.
   - Child item requirements: 10 bullet points, some are multi-sentence. Compress to one sentence each.
   - Pre-Exit Consistency Check: 6 prose bullets → 6 terse bullets.
   - Example flow: keep 1 compressed example.

2. **plan-mode-approved.md** — Compress phase descriptions, merge repeated instructions.

3. **plan-mode-uiux.md** — Already 53% cut. Audit for remaining prose.

4. **System prompts** — Many are small (commit message, title generation). Audit each. Compress only if verbose.

5. Preserve ALL Handlebars conditionals exactly. Plan-mode overlays are heavily conditional.
