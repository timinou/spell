* Context

The system prompt's section ordering buries Contract (5 inviolable rules) and Design Integrity
(5 engineering principles) at position ~75% of the stable section — after 2000+ tokens of tool
documentation. These are the agent's compass. They should prime behavior early, not arrive after
the agent has already absorbed LSP subcommands and AST pattern syntax.

Research-backed: critical instructions at START and END of prompt retain 20%+ better than
middle-positioned content (system-prompts skill, "Lost in the Middle" finding).

Architectural spec: =specs/system-prompt-architecture.md=

1. Move =# Contract= section from current position (after Environment, before Procedure)
   into the Identity section, placed AFTER =<stakes>=

   into the Identity section, placed AFTER Contract
3. Rename the ={{SECTION_SEPERATOR "Rules"}}= to ={{SECTION_SEPERATOR "Procedure"}}=
   since only the Procedure subsections remain after Contract and Design Integrity move out
4. Add two lines to the =<thinking-mode>= block:
   - After "Abbreviate freely. Go deep, write tight." add a new line:
     =Compression ≠ simplification.=
   - Change "Abbreviate freely." to "Abbreviate freely: fn, impl, cfg, dep, ret, sig, inv."

   separator should be preserved for readability.
6. Do NOT change any content within Contract or Design Integrity — only their position.
7. Do NOT move Procedure content — it stays where it is, just under a renamed separator.
