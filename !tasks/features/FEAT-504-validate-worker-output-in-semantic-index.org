* Problem
Semantic index construction trusts worker output shape without validation. Three gaps:
1. =build_semantic_index()= in =code_graph.rs= uses =zip(vectors)= at line 352, silently truncating when the worker returns fewer vectors than chunks.
2. Dimension consistency is not validated — =vectors.first().map_or(768, Vec::len)= at line 349 assumes all vectors share the first vector's dimension without checking.
3. =VectorIndex::search()= in =pi-code-vectors/src/index.rs= silently returns empty results on dimension mismatch (line 56-58) instead of reporting an error.

Since =PI_EMBEDDING_WORKER= is overrideable, all three are externally reachable.
