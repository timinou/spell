* Edge Cases
- Findings with no URL (empty string): skip dedup, always append
- Very long findingsJson (hundreds of findings): cap at 200 entries, oldest evicted
- QSettings size: findings metadata without contentBody stays under 1KB each, 200 entries = 200KB, within platform limits
- viewMode toggle with active filterTag: both filters apply simultaneously
- Enrichment of a curated finding by an auto-generated fetch: preserve curated='true'
- Enrichment of an auto finding by a curated agent finding: promote to curated='true'
