** Approach

The rewrite addresses three concerns:

1. *Compressed thinking becomes the default.* The =<thinking-mode>= block — symbols for grammar,
   structure for narrative — is no longer gated by caveman output mode. It renders for all sessions.
   Users opt out via =caveman.thinkingMode: "normal"=. This decouples reasoning style from output
   style. Template variable changes from =cavemanThinking= to =terseThinking=.

2. *Identity section gets full creative voice.* Behavior restores adversarial questions cut during
   compression. Code-integrity expands the outside-in framework with concrete failure modes. Design
   Integrity absorbs 6 philosophical principles from Procedure into 8 consolidated principles with
   expanded rationale.

3. *Procedure becomes purely operational.* The philosophical content in ## 5 "While Working" moves
   to Design Integrity. Procedure retains the opening sentence and operational instructions only.

** Key Decisions

- Design Integrity: 8 principles (was 5) — absorbed "one job one level", "fix at invariant", "inhabit call site"
- Contract, stakes, role, communication: unchanged
- caveman.md output template: unchanged
- Setting key =caveman.thinkingMode=: name unchanged (migration scope), description updated
