Cosmetic / low priority. After `delete` on a symbol, the declaration's span is removed but the trailing newline remains, leaving an empty line.

Repro: delete `dead` from `function keep(){}
function dead(){}
` → result `function keep(){}

` (blank line where `dead` was).

Fix: when a symbol/statement delete makes the line empty, extend the removed span to consume one trailing newline. Must preserve intentional blank-line separation between siblings and not over-consume when the deletion is mid-line. Low-risk, but verify against the sibling-deletion guard and the BUG-433/434 wrapper re-anchor (delete now targets the widest statement node).
