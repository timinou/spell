* Problem
=#pruneCompletedJobs()= (lines 305-316) sorts finished jobs by =startTime= (ascending) and removes the oldest. A long-running job that just completed can be pruned before a short job that completed much earlier. This makes "recent jobs" less truthful and can hide the freshest result under pressure.

** 3. Sort by =endTime= in =#pruneCompletedJobs()=
Change line 308:
#+begin_src typescript
// Before:
