* Fix

Add a sibling helper =resolveCwdRelativePath(cwd, p, opts)= in =path-utils.ts= that:
1. Calls =detectCwdPrefixDuplication=
2. Applies the BUG-360 coalesce-or-keep decision based on parent-dir existence
