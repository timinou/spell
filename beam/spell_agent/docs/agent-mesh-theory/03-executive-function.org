#+SUBTITLE: Cognitive-neurology backing for the record taxonomy and the black/* verbs

* Why this lens

The mesh needs a /principled/ answer to "what records, what verbs?" — not an
ad-hoc list. Cognitive neurology has spent decades decomposing exactly the
faculty we are building: *executive function* — the brain's machinery for
coordinating multiple sub-processes toward a goal under limited resources. That is
/precisely/ the mesh's job. So we borrow its decomposition as an independent
backing for the surface, and check that our verbs are complete and well-carved.

This is not metaphor-for-flavour. Two of the mappings are structurally exact
(claim ≡ basal-ganglia gating; watch ≡ frontopolar prospective memory), and they
/predicted/ verbs the pure-CS derivation under-weighted. Where the analogy is
loose, it is flagged and deferred — per the user's instruction, it is fine to
defer what is not already in line with the build; it remains as backing for future
reflection.

* The canonical decomposition

Two complementary models, unified for our purposes:

- *Miyake & Friedman's unity/diversity* — three separable-but-correlated core EFs:
  /updating/ working memory, /inhibition/, /shifting/ (set-switching).
- *Diamond's framework* — the three cores plus higher-order EFs built on them:
  /reasoning/, /planning/, /problem-solving/.
- plus the regulatory/monitoring layer from the /Norman–Shallice SAS/ (Supervisory
  Attentional System) and the /conflict-monitoring/ account of anterior cingulate.

From these, the executive functions relevant to a coordinating agent collective,
each with its dominant neural substrate and its mesh mapping:

| # | executive function | neural substrate | mesh record / verb | mapping rigor |
|---+--------------------+------------------+--------------------+---------------|

* The two exact mappings (the load-bearing ones)

** Claim ≡ basal-ganglia action gating

The basal ganglia implement /action selection/: many cortical motor plans compete;
the BG's *direct pathway* ("go") disinhibits the chosen plan while the *indirect
pathway* ("no-go") suppresses the rest, so /exactly one/ action crosses the
threshold into execution. This is not loosely "like" claiming — it is the same
computation:

| basal ganglia | mesh claim |
|---------------+------------|
| competing motor plans | competing =:claim= records for one work id |
| direct pathway disinhibits the winner | Lamport-min fold elects the winning claim |
| indirect pathway suppresses the rest | losing claimants /inhibit/ (stand down) |
| thalamic gate: one action proceeds | one session owns the work, others release |
| no homunculus decides — it is a network resolution | no lock/leader — a pure fold every contender computes |

The BG resolve selection /without a central decider/, by a distributed
winner-take-all over a shared substrate. That is exactly the consensus-free claim
arbitration of =02= §4.2. The neurology /validates/ the choice of "resolve by
fold, not by lock": brains gate actions this way precisely because central locking
does not scale to a body acting in real time.

** Watch ≡ frontopolar prospective memory

*Prospective memory* — remembering to do something /at a future moment or on a
future cue/ — is localized to frontopolar cortex (BA10), the most anterior PFC. It
maintains an intention in a latent state and fires it when the trigger condition is
met, /without occupying active attention in the meantime/. That is /exactly/
=black/watch=:

| prospective memory | mesh watch |
|--------------------+------------|
| a stored intention ("when I see the pharmacy, buy aspirin") | an =:intention= record (="when :finding{about: X}, run <ptc>"=) |
| latent — not in active WM, costs nothing while dormant | a record on the blackboard, no live process spinning |
| event-based cue fires it | a matching =post= fires the watch engine |
| fires, then completes | watch fires its action, burns fuel |

This mapping /predicted/ the surface that the user independently named — webhooks
and cross-agent unblocking are prospective memory at the collective scale: "when
the migration finding appears, proceed." The brain unblocks deferred intentions
through environmental cues; the mesh unblocks deferred agent actions through
blackboard cues. Same mechanism, same decoupling (the cue-poster need not know the
intention exists). =02= §5 trigger 3 is collective prospective memory.

* Why exactly these five record kinds (completeness check)

Run the EF decomposition against the record taxonomy and it /carves the same
joints/:

- a coordinating mind must *hold a goal* → =:goal= (EF 1)
- it must *update a shared working set with new facts* → =:finding= (EF 2, 10)
- it must *gate which sub-process owns which action* → =:claim= (EF 3, 5)
- it must *defer intentions to future conditions* → =:intention= (EF 6)
- it must *commit one collective decision when facts conflict irreducibly* →
  =:verdict= (EF 4, 9)

The monotone four (=goal=, =finding=, =claim=, =intention=) are the faculties a
/single/ executive uses to coordinate /its own/ sub-processes; the one
non-monotone kind (=verdict=) is the faculty a /collective/ needs when no single
executive presides. That is the Fork A / Fork B line, re-derived from neurology:
*Fork A is one executive coordinating its sub-processes; Fork B is a collective
synthesizing without a central executive.* The five kinds are necessary (each an
irreducible EF) and — to the resolution we are building at — sufficient.

* The fold is the prefrontal synthesis

=black/fold= is *integration* (EF 8) — rostral-PFC synthesis of sub-results into a
super-ordinate representation. The CALM insight and the neurology agree: synthesis
(non-monotone) is /localized/. In the brain it is rostral PFC; in Fork A it is the
parent; in Fork B it is the elected leader. You do not synthesize "everywhere at
once" — that is the homunculus fallacy in CS clothing (every node deciding
globally = divergence). One place integrates; the rest contribute monotone facts.

* What we defer (honest scope, kept as backing)

Per instruction, these EF mappings are /backing for future reflection/, not v1
build targets. Captured so the reasoning compounds:

- *EF 11 set-shifting* as a first-class verb. Today region re-targeting + =:goal=
  supersession cover it implicitly. A dedicated =black/shift= (atomically retire a
  goal and install its successor, re-pointing watchers) may earn its place later.
  Deferred.
- *EF 12 salience / attentional budget* as /learned/ allocation. Today
  =ParallelBudget= is a flat semaphore (uniform attention). A salience model
  (weight budget toward high-=:finding=-yield regions, like locus-coeruleus gain
  control) is a research direction, not v1. Deferred.
- *EF 9 collective decision* beyond crash-fault Raft. The neurology of /group/
  decision (drift-diffusion to a threshold, social-consensus dynamics) suggests
  richer protocols (weighted votes, confidence-pooling). Raft gives correct
  crash-fault agreement now; confidence-weighted collective decision is deferred
  research.
- *metacognition / SAS as a distinct layer.* The Norman–Shallice supervisory
  system that /monitors and overrides/ routine schemas maps to a future
  "meta-agent" that watches the mesh's own =:finding= stream for thrash and
  intervenes (re-budget, kill a cycle, re-goal). This is the natural home for a
  later "mesh supervisor". Deferred, but it is where EF 4 (conflict monitoring) and
  EF 12 (salience) would unify.

* One-line takeaways

- *claim = go/no-go gating* → resolve by distributed fold, never a lock (brains don't lock).
- *watch = prospective memory* → deferred intentions fired by environmental cues = webhooks + unblocking.
- *fold = prefrontal synthesis* → integration is localized; contribution is everywhere.
- *Fork A = one executive; Fork B = a collective with no central executive* → the
  monotone/non-monotone line is the with/without-executive line.
