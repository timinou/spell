* PRE-CODING CORRECTIONS (oracle MeshFinalCheck, agent://1-MeshFinalCheck)

** Seq replaces lamport everywhere (P2.1/P2.3)
- black/post: no Clock.tick_write — the store assigns seq atomically (FEAT-009).
- black/claim: fold claims_for(work) by argmin(SEQ, author_sid) over the
  store-assigned per-region total order (was argmin(lamport,sid)). Deterministic
  AND causally sound because seq is a real total order, not a per-session scalar.
- black/query: no tick_read; returns records by descending seq.

** won? is PROVISIONAL, documented (P2.3)
- black/claim's :won? is computed from the CALLER'S CURRENTLY-VISIBLE claim set,
  which single-node (Store.Memory, one ETS table) is authoritative but multi-node
  (Khepri local reads) may be stale. Document :won? as provisional:
  - monotone-effect work (the claim use case, DC-1): a transient double-win is
    harmless (dup findings collapse) — accept it.
  - expensive idempotent work: :settle_ms is REQUIRED (re-fold after the
    replication bound before acting).
  - non-deduped external effect: MUST route through decide, never claim.
- FEAT-013 (watcher) MUST NOT build unconditional exactly-once on claim alone —
  see FEAT-013 correction (node-plane micro-decide OR documented best-effort).

** Fork-A goal ownership is liveness-gated (P1.3)
- the FIRST :goal in a region records :owner = author session id.
- black/post of a SUBSEQUENT :goal (re-goal) is accepted ONLY if
  author == owner AND SessionRegistry.live?(owner) (lib/spell_agent/session_registry.ex).
  If the owner is dead, reject with a clear error directing the caller to promote
  the region to Fork-B and re-goal via decide. This prevents both two-active-goals
  (accept-any) and frozen-region (reject-all) when the ephemeral parent exits.

** Acceptance delta
- claim arbitration uses store seq; two concurrent same-work claims in one BEAM
  agree on exactly one winner by (seq, sid).
- a re-goal by a live owner succeeds; by a non-owner OR after the owner died, it
  is rejected with the documented message.
- :won? is documented provisional in the verb's doc string.
