* Problem
Hist realizes handles from OUTSIDE the runtime, on its own write path
(Hist.Realize.walk/1 wrapped around binds/result/sees in build_node, and around
the cont tape/memory in maybe_record_cont). The HandleStore session bucket cold-
evicts + tombstones parked terms, so Hist.Realize races the reaper: by the time
Recorder walks, a term may already be gone. Move B (Step.freeze/1) realizes at
the OWNER, synchronously post-run while the term is guaranteed live. Cut Hist
over to freeze-at-the-boundary and DELETE Hist.Realize entirely.
