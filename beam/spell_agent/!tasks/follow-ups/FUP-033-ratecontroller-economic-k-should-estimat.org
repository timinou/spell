* Summary
`SpellAgent.Hist.RateController` passes `remaining_turns = max_turns` (the per-mission turn ceiling) as the economic input `K` to `Mission.decide`. K is meant to be the EXPECTED number of future P-frames a keyframe amortizes over. Using the ceiling over-states it, so the pure-economic reduce rung (K > K*) can fire on a short/answer-only mission where a keyframe cannot amortize within the mission.

* Why it is bounded (not urgent)
Two mitigations already cap the downside (see rate_controller.ex comments):
1. The genuine-reduction guard rejects a reduce that does not strictly shrink the tape.
2. memoized_reduce makes a keyframe a ONE-TIME cost reused by every future mission at the same watermark, so a keyframe written on a short mission is amortized across the whole CONVERSATION, not just that mission's inner turns.
So the current behavior is safe and mostly-right; this is a tuning refinement, not a correctness fix.

* Fix (when tuning the controller)
Estimate turns-actually-left from a real signal instead of max_turns: e.g. a rolling average of missions-per-session, or a config `hist.expected_remaining_turns`. Feed that as K. Keep the pressure rungs (soft/hard overflow) unchanged \u2014 they do not read K and are always correct.
