* Summary (PLAN-027 M2)
`Cell.Registry` (frame-local, debounced, off-process, dependency-diffed — tuned for a cursor-keyed reactive query) and `DataSource.Registry` (query-clock, synchronous, heavy cross-cutting members) are TWO clocks of ONE idea: a named `data/*` binding produced by a frozen program, re-evaluated when its inputs change. Two registries = a parallel impl. Unify into one `Reactive.Binding` abstraction with a DECLARED clock strategy.

* The misalignment M2 closes
Two dep-diff engines, two registries, two moduledocs describing "a named data/* binding from a frozen query." The harmonious-code principle (one need = one implementation) says collapse them.

* Design (oracle-gated — touches the proven Cell path)
- One `Reactive.Binding` record: `%{name, program, clock, deps, resolved, debounce}` where `clock ∈ {:frame, :query}`.
  - `:frame` → the current Cell behavior: debounced, off-process Task, dep-diffed against the frame bag, generation-guarded.
  - `:query` → the current DataSource behavior: synchronous, resolved on reproject, cached.
- One registry (`Reactive.Registry`) storing both; the clock field selects the resolve strategy.
- `Cell.Clock.dirty/resolve` generalize to the binding's clock; DataSource.resolve_all becomes the `:query` strategy.
- `cell/def` and `data-source/register` become one verb `bind/def {name, program, clock}` (keep the old verbs as thin aliases for compat).
