* Summary
FEAT-045 shipped loop/continue as a Session-level trampoline: the mind ends a turn with `(return (tool/loop/continue {:prompt ...}))` and Session re-enters. This covers the "author my next prompt" use case with a safe, in-body design (no vendored-loop surgery). The DEFERRED extension: allow a continue to be emitted MID-program (not only as the terminal return value), e.g. `(loop/continue {:prompt ...})` as a control-flow signal like `return`/`fail`.

* Why deferred
Mid-program continue requires a ptc_runner patch: a new `{:__ptc_loop_continue__, prompt}` sentinel (parallel to `{:return_signal, _}` / `{:fail_signal, _}`) produced by the sandbox and handled by the loop's `handle_lisp_success` across ALL THREE transports (ptc_tool_call, text_mode, single-shot), plus its interaction with the per-turn budget check. That is a core-loop control-flow change with high blast radius that warrants its own focused wave + review cycle \u2014 not appropriate to rush as the final item of PLAN-025 W4.

* Design (from the FEAT-045 investigation)
- Sandbox: a `(loop/continue v)` special form emits `{:continue_signal, prompt}` (mirroring `return`/`fail`).
- Lisp.run: map it to `{:__ptc_loop_continue__, prompt}` (mirroring the __ptc_return__ mapping at lisp.ex ~914).
- Loop: `handle_lisp_success` matches the sentinel and re-enters instead of terminating; parity across ptc_tool_call.ex, text_mode.ex, single-shot.
- Budget: the continue MUST pass through check_termination (no bypass) so the turn/token ceiling still bounds it.
- SPELL_PATCHES.md: document as a new MOVE patch.

* Acceptance
- A PTC program can call `(loop/continue {:prompt ...})` NOT as the terminal value and have the loop re-enter.
- All three transports handle it identically.
- The continue cannot bypass the budget/turn ceiling.
- Reviewed (oracle/architecture) before merge \u2014 it is a core-loop change.
