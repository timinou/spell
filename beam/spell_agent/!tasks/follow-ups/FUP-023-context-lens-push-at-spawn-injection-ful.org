** PUSH-at-spawn (inject the bag as the child's data/* at spawn time)
The FEAT's headline "context is a LENS, not pushed" is satisfied by the verb
(pull). The richer ergonomic is to BUILD the bag at spawn and seed it into the
child's data/* so its prompt holes resolve against the cohort baseline WITHOUT a
runtime call \u2014 the cacheable immutable prefix. Needs:
- a :context arg on spawn-session ({:scope :siblings :where {...}}).
- ContextLens.build_context at spawn (handle has watermark+region+parent).
- thread the bag into the child Session.run as injected context (data/*), the same
  data/* seam PLAN-012's resolver uses. (Distinct from :inherit_memory which seeds
  the DEF-ENV; this seeds data/* context.)
